## Beimingwu: A Learnware Dock System

Beimingwu is an open-source research prototype for the **learnware paradigm**. It organizes existing well-trained models with specifications that characterize their capabilities, helping users identify and reuse suitable models for new tasks.

The platform supports model checking, organization, identification, and reuse. Its core Learnware package provides the foundation for research and development on learnware systems.
