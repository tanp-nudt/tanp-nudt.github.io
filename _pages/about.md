<p>I am a <strong>Postdoctoral Researcher (Deya Young Scholar)</strong> at the <strong>College of Science, National University of Defense Technology</strong>, where I have been working with Prof. <strong>Chenping Hou</strong> since February 2026.</p>
<p>I received my Ph.D. from the School of Artificial Intelligence, Nanjing University, in December 2025, advised by Prof. <a href="https://cs.nju.edu.cn/zhouzh/">Zhi-Hua Zhou</a>. I received my B.Sc. from the School of Information Science and Technology, University of Science and Technology of China, in June 2019.</p>
<p>My research interests include <strong>machine learning</strong> and <strong>data mining</strong>, with a particular focus on the <strong>learnware paradigm</strong>. My work explores how existing models can be identified, adapted, and reused across heterogeneous feature and label spaces to solve new tasks.</p>
