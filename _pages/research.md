## Research Interests

My research interests include **machine learning** and **data mining**, with a particular focus on the **learnware paradigm**: reusing existing well-trained models to solve new tasks.

## Project

### Beimingwu: A Learnware Dock System

Beimingwu is an open-source platform for sharing, identifying, and reusing well-trained machine learning models to solve new tasks. I contributed to its development as part of the Learnware-LAMDA team.
