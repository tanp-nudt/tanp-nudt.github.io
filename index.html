I am a Postdoctoral Researcher (Deya Young Scholar) at the College of Science, National University of Defense Technology, where I have been working with Prof. Chenping Hou since February 2026.

I received my Ph.D. from the School of Artificial Intelligence, Nanjing University, in December 2025, advised by Prof. Zhi-Hua Zhou. I received my B.Sc. from the School of Information Science and Technology, University of Science and Technology of China, in June 2019.

My research interests include machine learning and data mining, with a particular focus on the learnware paradigm. My work explores how existing models can be identified, adapted, and reused across heterogeneous feature and label spaces to solve new tasks.
